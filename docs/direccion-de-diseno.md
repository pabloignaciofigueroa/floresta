Scope: index.html — home + tienda + servicios + pedido (single-page storefront). Visitor mode: Persuade (with an Operate cart layer).
Audience: people in Castro/Chiloé (mostly mobile, from Instagram) sending flowers for a moment that matters. Action: add ramos to the cart and send the order via WhatsApp; or ask for a service (novias, condolencias, eventos).
Pinned by brief: "copy the style of the top 5 international florist sites" (Bloom & Wild, FLOWERBX, Farmgirl, UrbanStems, McQueens) -> canon played at full craft; roll 09e6ceda (degraded, no challengers) assigned grounded candidate 3, fused as the material layer.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A Bloom & Wild-grade flower shop that feels like Floresta's own counter in Castro. Refuses the discount-banner, countdown, grid-of-SKUs florist template.
OWN-WORLD: The shop facade as palette: deep facade green fields (#1E4636), mint plank (#D5E6DA), coral trim (#E9694C) as the single live accent, paper white (#F8F5EF) ground, kraft (#C7A57C) only on tags and tarjetas. Young Serif display, Hanken Grotesk text, a handwritten face only inside tarjetas. Kraft hang-tags carry prices; tarjeta cards carry messages. Real Instagram photos only.
STORY: Flores de verdad, hechas en Castro -> choose by moment -> see seasonal ramos -> services as equal doors -> the taller and the florist's hands -> how ordering works (3 steps) -> visit/contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed real ramo photo in a facade-green field, headline "Flores lindas, para momentos que importan.", one coral CTA "Elegir un ramo" + quiet WhatsApp link; slim bar "Despachos en Castro y alrededores".
SIGNATURE: the cart is a "tarjeta": you write the card message on a kraft tag, and the order is composed as a WhatsApp note in Floresta's voice.
RISK: canon familiarity; mitigated by the facade palette, kraft tags and real photos.
