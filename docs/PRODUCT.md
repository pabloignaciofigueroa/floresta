## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS (confirmed by owner, 2026-09-29). No build step; deployable to any static host (Netlify, GitHub Pages, cPanel).

## Users
- People in Castro and alrededores (Chiloé) who want to send flowers for a moment that matters: cumpleaños, amor, Día de la Madre, Día de la Novia, 21 de septiembre (flores amarillas), agradecimientos, condolencias.
- Mostly on mobile, arriving from Instagram (@florestaenchiloe) or Google ("flores en Castro").
- Secondary: people from outside Chiloé sending flowers to someone in Castro.

## Product Purpose
Online storefront and service showcase for FLORESTA, a florería at Esmeralda 198, Castro. Let visitors browse ramos and arreglos, understand the services (ramos personalizados, arreglos, regalos y detalles, condolencias y coronas, novias y eventos, plantas y orquídeas, despachos), and place an order. Success = more WhatsApp orders that arrive complete (producto, fecha, dirección, mensaje de tarjeta).

## Positioning
A local florería with decades of history in Castro ("30 años entregando arte a tu domicilio" per the Instagram bio), handmade in its own taller, with a real person behind every ramo. Warm, not transactional.
